# Podcast: Insights with Martin Motlík

One episode per Insights article, in English, self-hosted on GitHub Pages.
Design: the handoff "Insights Podcast" (article integration, podcast page,
cover "O · Ripple", tone InsightsLight).

**The feed is public** (`"feed_public": true` in `podcast/podcast.json`)
since 9 October 2026. The show is listed on
[Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/podcast/id6821022648) (ID 6821022648)
and [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/show/5qOblzcDjhTWfzlXcHRuRv); both links
live in `show.platforms` in `podcast/podcast.json`, which makes the icon
buttons (handoff "Icon buttons": 46 px with a tooltip in the podcast page
hero, 32 px next to the kicker of each article's episode card) and `sameAs`
in the `PodcastSeries` JSON-LD. A new platform needs its icon in `PLAT_ICON`
in `tools/build-podcast.py`.
The owner e-mail in the feed is `motlik.martin@icloud.com`. Setting
`feed_public` back to false removes `feed.xml` and every RSS link.

AI voices are disclosed in text only: in the show description and in each
episode's `disclosure` (feed, article, JSON-LD). There is no spoken note in
the audio; if Apple ever asks for one (guideline 1.11), add it at the end of
the episode so the chapter times stay put.

### "Get new episodes" (e-mail)

The button and the form appear once `show.subscribe_endpoint` in
`podcast/podcast.json` holds a form endpoint, e.g. Formspree
(`https://formspree.io/f/<id>`; each sign-up arrives as an e-mail). Without
it the page shows only "Play latest episode". The form posts `email` with
`Accept: application/json` and shows "Subscribed ✓" or an error; new
episodes are announced by hand to the collected addresses. Mention the
sign-up in the privacy information.

### Listing on Apple Podcasts, Spotify & co.

1. Set `"feed_public": true` in `podcast/podcast.json` and run
   `python3 tools/build-i18n.py`. That publishes `podcast/feed.xml` and adds
   the autodiscovery links and `webFeed` in JSON-LD. Commit and deploy.
2. Submit `https://martinmotlik.com/podcast/feed.xml` in Apple Podcasts
   Connect and Spotify for Creators (your accounts; they verify ownership via
   the e-mail in the feed).
3. Once approved, put the Apple and Spotify show links in `show.platforms`
   and rebuild (done for both).

The animated cover on the podcast page is the same design in HTML/CSS
(`.cv` in `insights/podcast/index.html`). The portrait is two layers of the
same pixels: the 200 px disc holds the blue and the photo under one clip
edge (so the shoulders follow the circle), and the head above it is the
photo again, fading out between 95 and 115 px of its box. Keep the generator
and the page in step if the design changes.

Everything else is generated by `tools/build-podcast.py`, which
`tools/build-i18n.py` runs first:

- `podcast/feed.xml` (when public): Apple / Spotify tags plus Podcasting 2.0
  (GUID, transcript, chapters, locked feed), a cover per episode
- on the podcast page: the latest episode with its player, older episodes,
  the e-mail opt-in, and the `PodcastSeries` / `PodcastEpisode` JSON-LD
- in the article: the episode card `#episode` (chapters, synced transcript,
  AI note), "Also a podcast" under the contents, "Listen from m:ss"
  under each mapped H2, and the `PodcastEpisode` JSON-LD
- on both: the floating mini player and the episode data for the player

## Adding an episode

1. Put the files in place: `assets/podcast/epNNN-<slug>.mp3` and `.vtt`, and
   the transcript as `podcast/episodes/epNNN-<slug>.md` with chapters as
   `### Title [m:ss]` and lines as `**Name** [m:ss]: text`.
2. Draw the cover with `tools/podcast-cover.html` (tag `EP NN`) and save the
   3000 px PNG as `Sources/originals/podcast/cover-epNN-3000.png` (on the
   `site-range` dev server, `exportCover('EP NN', 3000, 'image/png', 0, 'cover-epNN-3000.png')`
   saves it to `Sources/exports/`). Then `node tools/podcast-covers.mjs`
   writes the 3000 px JPG and the 96 / 144 / 264 px AVIF + JPG renditions
   (and the show cover's 3000 / 1200 px JPGs from `cover-3000.png`).
3. Add the episode to `podcast/podcast.json`: number, slug, title, article,
   category, publish date, duration in whole seconds, cover, teaser (podcast
   page), summary (JSON-LD, feed), note, disclosure, and `h2`: which H2 (`id`)
   gets which chapter start, in seconds.
4. In the article, add the markers `<!-- podcast:start -->` /
   `<!-- podcast:end -->` before the sources, `toc-pod` after the contents,
   `pod-mini` and `pod-data` before the scripts, the byline player (`#vo`),
   `podcast.css` and `podcast.js`, as in the first article. Add the labels
   (`pod_*`, `also_*`, `chip_at`, the player strings in `ui`) to its
   `i18n/cs.json` and `de.json`.
5. On `/insights/`, add the "Podcast · N min" tag to the article's cards.
6. Translate the teaser and the date for the podcast page in
   `insights/podcast/i18n/cs.json` and `de.json` (`text`).
7. `python3 tools/build-i18n.py`, check, commit, deploy. Once the feed is
   public, Apple and Spotify pick new episodes up by themselves (usually
   within hours).

The publish date must not be in the future when the feed goes live, and the
episode `guid` (built from the slug) must never change, or apps show the
episode twice.

## Audio spec

Delivered by the production session as MP3, which every podcast app plays.
Checked here, not re-encoded (macOS has no MP3 encoder, and re-encoding
would only lose quality):

| | Target | Episode 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Format | MP3, 44.1 kHz | MP3 stereo, 192 kbps, 44.1 kHz |
| Integrated loudness | −16 LUFS (Apple / Spotify) | −16.3 LUFS |
| Peak | ≤ −1 dBFS | −1.7 dBFS (sample peak) |
| Loudness range | 3–6 LU for speech | 2 LU |
| Length | — | 14:08, 20.4 MB |

Loudness measured with a BS.1770 meter written for this site (K-weighting,
400 ms blocks, −70 / −10 gates); it reads the earlier voice-over within
0.05 LU of its known value.

## Hosting notes

GitHub Pages answers byte-range requests (206), which Apple requires and the
chapter links need for seeking. Files under 100 MB are fine in the repository;
at roughly 20 MB per episode, keep an eye on the repository size after about
50 episodes. There are no download statistics: plays on the website can be
counted in GA4, but listens in Apple Podcasts or Spotify only show in their
own dashboards (Apple Podcasts Connect, Spotify for Creators) after the show
is claimed there with the owner e-mail in the feed.
