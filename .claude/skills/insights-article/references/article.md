# The article: writing and building

Reference page: `insights/ai-visibility-for-aec/index.html`. Its first draft
was a standalone HTML file (`_insights/ai-visibility-for-aec/drafts/`); the
published page is that text moved into the site template.

## Writing rules

- **One article, one idea**, told through Martin's own work (engineering-
  software websites, localization for the U.S., Canada and Europe, AI in
  AEC). The reader is an AEC marketer, web lead or engineer who decides
  about content.
- **Title**: the H1 in sentence case, pattern "Claim: what it means"
  ("Be the source: getting AEC data ready for AI search"), ≤ 60 characters.
  The EN `<title>` has the same words in U.S. Title Case ("Be the Source:
  Getting AEC Data Ready for AI Search"); CS/DE titles stay sentence case and
  may drop a word to fit 60 characters. No "| Martin Motlík" suffix. A short name (2–4 words) for the
  breadcrumb ("Be the source").
- **Dek** under the H1: one or two sentences, the promise of the article.
- **Lead** (`p.lead`): the problem in the reader's world, then why Martin is
  the one writing about it.
- **Sections**: 5–8 H2s, each a claim or a question, each with a stable `id`
  (short, English, kebab-case: `ai-search`, `load-path`). The ids are also
  the podcast chapter anchors (`h2` map in `podcast.json`).
- **Answer first**: every section opens with its point; every passage must
  stand on its own (AI systems quote passages).
- **Concrete over abstract**: one real example from Martin's work per key
  idea (the timber page, the Vancouver IP localization). Name standards,
  tools and markets explicitly.
- **Practical part**: at least one thing the reader can do or check
  themselves (steps, a command, a kit). Date anything that ages (a robots.txt
  kit says "as of Oct 2026").
- **Sources**: numbered `[1]`, `[2]`… in the text, a list at the end with
  title, publisher, month and year. Prefer primary sources (the keynote, the
  official documentation), never a blog that quotes them.
- **Key takeaway** box near the end: one or two sentences.
- **Closing**: "Next step" with the LinkedIn CTA, as in article #1.
- **Length**: 1,800–2,500 words (article #1: "8 min read"). Read time =
  words / 230, rounded.
- **Language**: U.S. English, first person, short sentences, no hype words
  ("revolutionary", "game-changer"), no rhetorical filler, no em-dash chains.
- **Four tags** (`article:tag`, the build expects exactly four) and one
  category (article #1: "AI in AEC").

## Building the page

Copy `insights/ai-visibility-for-aec/index.html` to `insights/<slug>/index.html`
and replace content, keeping the structure:

1. **Head**: GA4 consent snippet, `<title>`, description (≤ 160 characters),
   canonical, hreflang en/cs/de/x-default, Open Graph (image = the 1200×630
   OG crop, `og:image:alt`), `article:published_time` / `modified_time` /
   `author` / `section`, four `article:tag`, Twitter card, fonts, `podcast.css`,
   AVIF LCP preload of the hero.
2. **JSON-LD `@graph`**: `TechArticle` (`@id …/#article`, headline,
   description, `inLanguage`, dates, author/publisher `{"@id":
   "https://martinmotlik.com/#martin-motlik"}`, images, `isPartOf`
   `/insights/#blog`, `articleSection`, `keywords`, `about`, `wordCount`,
   `citation[]` for every source, `workTranslation` cs/de), the `Person`
   node, `BreadcrumbList` (Home › Insights › short name). The `PodcastEpisode`
   node is generated by `build-podcast.py`; don't write it by hand.
3. **Hero**: crumbs, category pill, H1, dek, byline (avatar, name, role, the
   podcast player `#vo`, date + read time), cover `<picture>` with
   `fetchpriority="high"`.
4. **TOC aside**: "On this page" links to every H2, the `toc-pod` markers,
   the share block.
5. **Article**: mobile TOC, lead, sections (`.h2-row` around each H2 so the
   build can add "Listen from m:ss"), quotes, steps, code blocks, key
   takeaway, the `podcast:start/end` markers, sources, closing, share row,
   four tags, author card, "All insights →".
6. **After `</main>`**: `#sr-live`, `pod-mini` and `pod-data` markers,
   `podcast.js`, the page script.
7. Every translatable block carries `data-k="<section>.<tag><n>"`, short
   labels `data-i18n`, attributes `data-i18n-attr` (see `translation.md`).

## Images

1. Full-resolution hero in `Sources/originals/images/<name>.jpg` (≥ 1672 px
   wide, landscape; Martin in the scene, as in article #1).
2. Register it in `tools/build-images.mjs`: `IMAGES` with role `gallery`, and
   a `CROPS` entry `{slug: '<name>-og', ratio: 1200/630, widths: [1200]}`.
3. `npm run images`, then `node tools/picture.mjs <name> --sizes "(min-width: 1128px) 1032px, calc(100vw - 48px)" --alt "…" --eager` for the markup.
4. Alt text describes the scene; translate it (`cover_alt`).

## The rest of the site

- `/insights/`: the new article becomes `a.featured`; the previous one moves
  into the grid as `a.card[data-cat=…]`. Update the filter-chip counts, the
  `Blog` JSON-LD `blogPost` list, and `insights/i18n/{cs,de}.json`
  (`aN_title`, `aN_ex`, `date_aN`, read time, alts). Add the
  "Podcast · N min" tag when the episode exists.
- `llms.txt` → "## Insights": `[Title](url): Published YYYY-MM-DD.` + a dense
  factual summary + "Key points: …" + "Topics: …", the CS and DE title lines,
  the link list and the language table. Keep it in step with the visible
  copy.
- `tools/build-i18n.py`: `PAGES`, `URLS` (slugs), `SITEMAP` (lastmod).
- Once two articles exist, add "Keep reading" to both.

## Before article #2: generalize the build

The build was written for one article. Before adding the second one, make it
article-agnostic (its own branch, verify that article #1's output is
byte-identical except for intended changes):

- `tools/build-i18n.py`: the `ARTICLE` constant and `build_article()`
  (one article, `citation[0]`); the literal share title
  "Be the source: getting AEC data ready for AI search" (and the ≥12 URL /
  ≥6 title assertions); `word_count()` with fixed section ids; the
  `article:tag` regex `{4}`; `build_list()` updating only `blogPost[0]`;
  `ENGLISH_MARKERS` with "8 min read" / "Oct 6, 2026".
- `tools/build-podcast.py`: `HERO = '/assets/img/ai-search-hero'` for the
  latest-episode card → take each episode's article hero.
- Read article data (path, slugs, hero, share title, sections) from one
  registry per article instead of constants.
