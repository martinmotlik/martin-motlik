# Brief: Be the source: getting AEC data ready for AI search

*Reconstructed after publishing, from the article, the episode and the git history, as the reference for later briefs.*

## Topic and angle

- **Topic**: how AEC companies make their technical data easy for AI search to find, read and verify.
- **From Martin's own work**: localizing engineering-software websites for the U.S., Canada and Europe since 2021; the timber design page rebuilt from Eurocode to NDS / CSA O86 (text, screenshots, image texts); IP-based localization of one URL for several markets.
- **Why now**: Google I/O, May 2026: AI Overviews past 2.5 billion monthly users, AI Mode past one billion, the two merging [1].

## Reader

- **Who**: marketing and web leads at AEC software vendors, manufacturers and engineering firms.
- **Their question**: "Why doesn't ChatGPT / Google's AI name us, when we rank on page one?"
- **What they can do after reading**: run three checks (crawler fetch, robots.txt and CDN, ten real questions), use the robots.txt kit, start with one product or service page.

## Core line

> A page can be perfectly correct and still invisible.
