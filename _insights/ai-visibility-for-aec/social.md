## LinkedIn: long

```
For twenty years, a good product page with sharp photos was the finish line. Today, it's raw material.

Google now answers engineering questions before showing a single link. Whether an engineer ever sees your page depends on whether an AI system can read it, trust it and quote it.

My work on engineering-software websites for the U.S., Canada and Europe taught me this long before anyone talked about AI search. Localization showed me that a page can be perfectly correct and still invisible, because it answers a question nobody in that market asks.

One example from my own work: a timber design page written in the language of Eurocode. Accurate and thorough, but engineers in the U.S. and Canada design to NDS and CSA O86. So I rebuilt the page, the screenshots and the image texts around their design standards and their questions.

In my new article, I share what that experience means for AI visibility:
→ how a single fact travels from your server into an AI answer
→ why AI crawlers can miss content that ranks fine in Google
→ my rules for AI-ready data, and practical checks you can run yourself

📖 Read the article, or 🎧 listen to the podcast episode, where my co-host Sarah brings a practical case and we take it apart together:
https://martinmotlik.com/insights/ai-visibility-for-aec/

#AEC #AISearch #TechnicalSEO
```
